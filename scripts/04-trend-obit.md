# 04 — Trend obituary

**Format:** Mock-eulogy for a dying trend. Comedic tone, fast pace. Easy to shoot once a week from your phone.

## Title (pick one)
- `Trend obit: [X] (RIP)`
- `Pour one out — [X] is officially dead`
- `[X] died this week. Cause of death:`

## 30-second beat sheet

| Time | What's happening | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | **Hook.** Black screen. Bell tolls (sfx). On-screen: `RIP: [X]` in serif font. | "Pour one out. [X] is officially dead." |
| 3–7s | Cut to face, deadpan. On-screen birth/death dates: `[BORN] – [DIED]` | "Born [month]. Peaked [date]. Time of death: [date]." |
| 7–13s | **Cause of death 1.** On-screen: `1. [reason]` | "Killed by: [overuse / brand co-opting / a cringe celebrity post / etc.]." |
| 13–19s | **Cause of death 2.** On-screen: `2. [reason]` | "Made worse by: [specific moment that was the death rattle]." |
| 19–25s | **Cause of death 3.** On-screen: `3. [reason]` | "Final nail: [the cringe attempt that nobody can unsee]." |
| 25–30s | Direct address. On-screen: `Which trend dies next?` | "Which trend is next? Comment your guess. Following back the best ones." |

## Visual notes
- Black background + serif text = funeral vibes. Lean into the bit.
- B-roll: the brand logos / celebrity reposts that killed the trend. Stock or fair-use commentary clips only.
- Music: a quiet, lonely piano fading out. YouTube Audio Library has options labeled "sad" or "reflective."
- Tone: **deadpan**, not laughing. The joke is the seriousness.

## Description template

```
Trend obituary — [X] (RIP)

Cause of death:
1. [reason]
2. [reason]
3. [final nail]

Which trend dies next? Best guess in the comments gets a follow back.

#shorts #trendobit #trendsdying #trending #fyp
```

## Why this format works
Negative/dying-trend takes have higher comment rates than positive ones — people argue about whether it's actually dead. Comments compound the algorithm push. Don't be mean about creators; mock the *trend*, not the people doing it.
