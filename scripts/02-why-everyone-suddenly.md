# 02 — Why everyone is suddenly [doing X]

**Format:** Explainer. Pick a single trend everyone's seeing right now and explain *why* it's everywhere. Higher retention than predictions because the audience already cares.

## Title (pick one)
- `Why everyone is suddenly [X] (it's not what you think)`
- `The real reason [X] is on every feed right now`
- `[X] is everywhere. Here's the actual reason.`

## 30-second beat sheet

| Time | What's happening | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | **Hook.** Show 3 quick clips of the trend, layered. On-screen: `Why is [X] EVERYWHERE?` | "If your feed is full of [X] this week, here's the real reason." |
| 3–8s | Cut to your face. On-screen: `It's not random.` | "It's not random. Three things converged." |
| 8–14s | **Trigger event.** Show a date stamp / screenshot of the originating post or person. | "[Date]: [creator/event] dropped [thing]. That was the spark." |
| 14–20s | **Algorithm vector.** Show platform logo. | "Then [platform] pushed it — it hit the [demographic] feed at [time]." |
| 20–26s | **The twist.** Lean in. On-screen: `And the part nobody mentions:` | "And here's what nobody's saying: [X] is actually a recycled [old trend] from [year]." |
| 26–30s | Direct address. On-screen: `Trends explained, not just shown.` | "Follow if you want trends explained, not just shown. New short tomorrow." |

## Visual notes
- Use timeline graphics — three dated tiles (date + event) animating in.
- Pull screenshots or short clips for the trigger event. **Use stock or your own footage**, not the original creator's clip — credit them in the description if you reference them.
- Music: mid-tempo, suspenseful build. CapCut's "investigation" or "reveal" presets fit.

## Description template

```
Why is [X] suddenly everywhere? The actual reason:

→ [Date]: [trigger event]
→ Platform amplification: [platform / demographic]
→ The recycled bit: [old trend it borrows from]

Credit: [original creator / source if applicable]

#shorts #trending #[topic] #explainer #fyp
```

## Pitfall to avoid
Do not reupload the original creator's clip. Use stock B-roll, your own footage, or a short licensed snippet with credit. Reused content is the #1 reason new channels get demonetized.
