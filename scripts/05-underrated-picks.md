# 05 — Underrated → going viral in 30 days

**Format:** Rapid-fire 5 picks. Discovery flex — you spotted them first. Drives saves and follows because viewers want to "be early."

## Title (pick one)
- `5 underrated [niche] picks that will be everywhere in 30 days`
- `These 5 are about to blow up. You're early.`
- `Save this — 5 picks before they hit the algorithm`

## 30-second beat sheet

| Time | What's happening | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | **Hook.** Big on-screen text: `Under [N] followers — for now.` | "These five have under [N] followers right now. They won't in a month." |
| 3–7s | **Pick 1.** Full-screen card: name, handle, follower count, niche. | "[Name 1] — [niche]. [One-line reason: 'Best [thing] I've seen this year.']" |
| 7–11s | **Pick 2.** Same card style. | "[Name 2] — [niche]. [Reason]." |
| 11–15s | **Pick 3.** | "[Name 3] — [niche]. [Reason]." |
| 15–19s | **Pick 4.** | "[Name 4] — [niche]. [Reason]." |
| 19–24s | **Pick 5.** | "[Name 5] — [niche]. [Reason]." |
| 24–28s | Cut to face. On-screen: `Which one are you betting on?` | "Which are you betting on? Comment 1 through 5." |
| 28–30s | On-screen: `Follow @Trendiest — early picks weekly.` | "Follow. New picks every week." |

## Visual notes
- Card template: handle on top, follower count in the corner, one-line reason centered, mini-screenshot of their best post in the background.
- Make the cards in Canva once, reuse the template every week — just swap text and screenshots.
- Tight cuts: 3–5 seconds per pick. Snappy beat-drop on each card transition.
- Music: high-energy, builds with each pick. CapCut "trending" presets fit.

## Compliance notes
- Always credit the people you feature (handle on screen + tagged in description).
- Don't show their video clips beyond a 1–2 second screenshot — fair use commentary, no full re-uploads.
- Many creators love getting featured. DM the bigger ones first; they may collab back.
