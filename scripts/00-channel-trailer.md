## Production checklist (phone-and-face, ~20 minutes total)

### Setup (2 min)
- [ ] Stand or sit **facing a window** — daylight is the best lighting, free.
- [ ] Phone in **vertical orientation**, propped at chest height (book stack works).
- [ ] **Front camera**, 1080p, 60fps if your phone supports it.
- [ ] Quiet room. If background noise, use earbuds with a built-in mic.

### Record (8 min)
- [ ] Record each row in the table above as a **separate clip** (5 clips total).
- [ ] Speak slowly. Smile slightly on the hook. Confident, not loud.
- [ ] Do **2–3 takes** of each clip. You'll pick the best in editing.

### Edit in CapCut (free, 8 min)
1. Open CapCut → **New Project** → import all your clips.
2. Trim each clip to keep only the best take. Total length: 28–30 seconds.
3. **Auto-captions:** tap `Text` → `Auto Captions` → English. Pick a bold style (CapCut's "Bold" or "Highlight" preset).
4. **Music:** tap `Audio` → `Sounds` → search "trending pop" or "upbeat." Pick a track, set volume to **15%** (background only).
5. **Aspect ratio:** confirm 9:16.
6. Export → **1080p, 60fps**.

### Upload (2 min)
1. Open **studio.youtube.com** on your phone or laptop.
2. Click **Create** (top-right) → **Upload videos** → drop in the export.
3. Title: paste from above.
4. Description: paste from above.
5. **Audience:** "No, it's not made for kids."
6. **Visibility:** Public.
7. Click **Publish**.

### Pin it (1 min)
1. After publishing, go to your channel → click the trailer → click the **⋯** menu.
2. **Pin to channel.** Now every visitor sees it first.
