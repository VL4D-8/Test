# 03 — Trend autopsy

**Format:** Anatomy of a viral hit. Walk through *what made it work* — hook, format, timing. This positions you as the analyst, not just the reporter.

## Title (pick one)
- `Trend autopsy: what actually made [X] go viral`
- `[X] hit [N]M views in [N] days. Here's why it worked.`
- `Reverse-engineering the [X] trend`

## 30-second beat sheet

| Time | What's happening | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | **Hook.** Show a stat counter ticking up: `0 → [N]M views`. On-screen: `[X] hit [N]M in [N] days.` | "[X] hit [N] million views in [N] days. Let's autopsy it." |
| 3–7s | **The hook breakdown.** Show the trend's first 1.5 seconds + a callout box. | "Hook: [the actual opening line]. It works because it [violates expectation / poses a question / shows the punchline upfront]." |
| 7–14s | **The format.** Whiteboard or screen-recording style with arrows. | "Format: [pattern A] → [pattern B] → [payoff]. Same shape as [comparable past trend], just swapped [variable]." |
| 14–22s | **The timing.** Show a small chart climbing. | "Timing: it dropped [day/event]. Audience was [emotional state]. The trend caught the wave, not the other way around." |
| 22–28s | **The lesson for creators.** Direct address. | "Steal this: [one transferable tactic — e.g. front-load the payoff, end on a loop, etc.]." |
| 28–30s | On-screen: `Follow for more autopsies.` | "Follow. New autopsy every week." |

## Visual notes
- This one rewards motion graphics. Use CapCut text presets that animate in/out.
- Show the trend in a **picture-in-picture** corner box, not full-screen. You're commenting, not replaying.
- Whiteboard-style arrows and circles work well — Procreate or Canva can export PNG overlays.

## Description template

```
Why [X] worked, in 30 seconds:

▸ Hook: [hook formula]
▸ Format: [format pattern]
▸ Timing: [why now]
▸ Steal-it: [transferable tactic]

What trend should I autopsy next? Drop it below.

#shorts #trendanalysis #creatortips #trending #fyp
```
